Features

Lumenkorn runs entirely on your computer. The AI features are optional and work offline once you have downloaded the models.

  • Library
  • RAW development
  • Auto in your style
  • AI
  • Statistics
  • Map
  • Photo album
  • More

Library

Lumenkorn keeps your folder structure. You add folders and the photos stay exactly where they are. Files are only copied when you import from a memory card.

  • When importing from a card, Lumenkorn sorts the photos into dated folders (2025\2025.10.03_Occasion) and names them by capture time. Every copy is checked byte by byte before the card can be cleared. If the camera clock was wrong, you can correct it during the import.
  • New, changed and moved files are picked up automatically. Moved photos are recognized by their content, so ratings and edits stay with them.
  • Projects group several folders, even from different drives.
  • Rate with stars, pick or reject, and add keywords. The search covers file names, folders, camera, lens, keywords, people and places.
  • The file type filter in the toolbar shows only certain formats, such as only ARW or only JPEG, or all RAW files at once. It also applies in Develop.
  • For culling, reject a photo with one key and move straight on to the next. Rejected photos can then go to the trash in one go.
  • Compare up to 12 photos side by side with synchronized zoom.
  • Lumenkorn reads JPEG, PNG, TIFF, WebP, OpenEXR and RAW files from Canon, Nikon, Sony, Fujifilm, Panasonic, OM System, Pentax, DNG and more.

RAW development

Editing is non-destructive, the way you know it from other programs. Only your settings are saved and the original file stays as it is. For RAW files Lumenkorn follows darktable's scene-referred workflow, which was translated to Rust for this.

  • White balance, exposure, contrast, highlights, shadows, whites, blacks, clarity, dehaze, vibrance, saturation and sharpening.
  • HSL for eight color ranges with an eyedropper, color grading with color wheels, and a tone curve for RGB and each channel.
  • Black and white with its own gray mix.
  • Lens correction for distortion, color fringes and vignetting, either from the camera's own data or from the Lensfun profiles for about 1,500 lenses.
  • Crop and rotate, with automatic horizon leveling.
  • Local adjustments with gradients, brush, color range and AI masks.
  • History with undo, snapshots, and settings you can copy to many photos.
  • Export to JPEG, 16-bit TIFF or PNG.
  • With a graphics card (DirectX 12 or Vulkan) the sliders respond right away. Without a suitable card, the processor takes over.

Auto in your style

Clicking Auto sets the tone so the photo looks balanced. If you keep editing afterwards, Lumenkorn notes where you differ from that suggestion. It does this separately for each camera and for RAW and JPEG:

  1. From about 10 to 20 edits with one camera, it applies a fixed correction, such as “blacks −12”.
  2. From about 50, the correction depends on how bright the photo is.
  3. From about 300, it also takes into account what the photo shows.

A slider is only adjusted when that demonstrably brings Auto closer to your own results. In the settings you can see what has been learned, switch it off or reset it. None of this leaves your computer.

AI

For the AI features, Lumenkorn downloads open models when you ask it to. After that they run offline on the processor or the graphics card (AMD, NVIDIA and Intel). If you don't want AI, everything else still works.

Masking

Lumenkorn finds the main subject, the sky, people and animals. For people it can be more precise, for example only the hair, eyes, lips or skin. Click on any other object and it becomes a mask too. Masks can be added to and subtracted from each other.

Noise reduction

The noise reduction removes noise and keeps fine structures such as feathers. Color noise gets a pass of its own. With AI repair, dust spots, power lines, signs or people disappear from the picture.

Keywords and search

Lumenkorn suggests keywords for place, subject, light, weather and style. When you search, you can use your own words: type “mountains” and you get mountains, even if nobody ever tagged those photos.

People

Faces are found and grouped in the background. Once you have named a few people, Lumenkorn suggests names for other photos. People can be sorted into your own groups such as family or friends.

Statistics

Which lens do you use most, and which focal lengths do you actually shoot at? The statistics cover the photos currently shown, so a folder, a project or a search result. You see cameras, lenses, focal lengths, apertures, shutter speeds, ISO values, keywords, people, countries, file types and ratings, plus the months and times of day the photos were taken.

Click an entry to filter by it. That way you can see, for example, which focal lengths you use with a particular lens.

Map

  • The map shows the photos of the current folder, project or search result on an OpenStreetMap map.
  • GPS data from camera or phone is read automatically. Photos without a location can get one through the place search, by dragging them onto the map, or from a phone photo taken a few minutes earlier.
  • Every location gets a place name you can search for.
  • The map needs the internet, and you can switch it off completely (see Privacy).

Photo album

A few clicks turn a project or folder into a print-ready photo album. Choose a theme (travel, wedding, family, nature and others, or describe your own), a page size and how many photos it should have.

  • Lumenkorn picks sharp, well-exposed photos. Bursts and near-identical shots count once, unless different people are in them. Photos with four or five stars always go in.
  • Pages are filled in the order the photos were taken, using the layout that crops the least.
  • Date and place appear where a new day or place begins. You can add your own text anywhere.
  • If you like, a map page shows the countries you visited and a pin for every place, along with cities, mountains, rivers and lakes. It is built into the program and needs no internet.
  • Swap, move or remove photos with a click. At the end Lumenkorn saves a 300 dpi PDF with bleed and every page as a JPG.

More

  • HDR, panorama and focus stacking from several photos, also handheld. The result is a 16-bit file you keep editing like a RAW.
  • Nextcloud: right-click a project to upload its exported photos to a folder in your own Nextcloud and share it as a link, with a password and expiry date if you like. Uploading again only sends new and changed photos, and the link stays the same. Shared projects get a cloud icon in the sidebar. You log in through the browser, and Lumenkorn gets its own app password that you can revoke in Nextcloud at any time.
  • Keyboard shortcuts for almost everything, and you can change them in the settings.
  • AI agents such as Claude or ChatGPT can operate Lumenkorn through a built-in MCP interface if you switch it on. They can search, rate, develop and export, but not delete anything.
  • Updates are offered by Lumenkorn itself, and each new version is checked by its signature before it is installed.
  • The interface is available in English and German.
