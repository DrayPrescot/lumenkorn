Published methods

Lumenkorn implements these methods itself, partly by translating code (darktable).

  • From darktable (translated to Rust): RCD demosaicing (Luis Sanz Rodríguez), the highlight reconstruction “inpaint opposed” (garagecoder and Iain from the G'MIC team, Hanno Schwalm), color calibration with CAT16 and the sigmoid tone mapper.
  • Markesteijn demosaicing for X-Trans (Frank Markesteijn), through rawler.
  • Oklab and Oklch (Björn Ottosson) for HSL, color ranges and color grading.
  • Dark channel prior (Kaiming He et al.) as the basis of dehaze.
  • Lens models of Lensfun/Hugin, the DNG opcodes WarpRectilinear and FixVignetteRadial, the meaning of Sony's correction data as worked out by darktable and RawTherapee, and Panasonic's distortion data following Raphaël Rigo (trou/panasonic-rw2).
  • Harris corners, BRIEF descriptors, RANSAC and Hartley normalization for aligning photos; Laplacian pyramids (Burt and Adelson) for focus stacking.

Development and testing

Used to build and test Lumenkorn: TypeScript, Vite, svelte-check, Tauri CLI, Mesa, tempfile, OpenImageIO-images.

The README on GitHub has the complete list with every library. In the app, ⓘ (About Lumenkorn) at the bottom left shows it too.
