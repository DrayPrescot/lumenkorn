Hosting

This website is hosted by Cloudflare, Inc., 101 Townsend St, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA (Cloudflare Pages). When you open a page, your browser sends technical data that Cloudflare processes to deliver the page and to protect it against attacks: IP address, date and time, the page requested, browser type and operating system, and the referring page. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in a secure and reliable website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

We do not keep server logs ourselves and do not see individual IP addresses. Cloudflare only gives us aggregated numbers, such as how often the pages were visited.

We have a data processing agreement with Cloudflare. For transfers to the USA, Cloudflare is certified under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework (adequacy decision of the European Commission of 10 July 2023); the agreement also includes the European Commission's standard contractual clauses. Details: Cloudflare privacy policy.

Encryption

The website is only available over an encrypted connection (HTTPS), so third parties cannot read what you send when opening a page.

No cookies, no tracking

This website sets no cookies, uses no analytics or tracking and loads nothing from other servers; fonts and images come from this website itself. If you switch the language, your choice is stored in your browser's local storage (lang) so the start page does not switch back. It never leaves your browser and you can delete it at any time.

Contact by email

If you email us, we use your address and message only to answer your request. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in answering it (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). The email is deleted once the matter is settled, unless the law requires us to keep it.

Emails are usually not end-to-end encrypted, so it is better not to send anything confidential by email.

GitHub

Downloads, source code, bug reports and discussions are hosted by GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P Kelly Jr St, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA. Retention

We keep personal data only as long as needed for its purpose. We store nothing from this website ourselves; how long Cloudflare keeps technical data is described in Cloudflare's privacy policy. Emails are deleted as described above.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to

  • access the data stored about you (Art. 15),
  • have incorrect data corrected (Art. 16),
  • have data erased (Art. 17) or its processing restricted (Art. 18),
  • data portability (Art. 20), and
  • object to processing based on legitimate interests (Art. 21).

An email to info@lumenkorn.com is enough. You can also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR). The competent authority is the Bavarian Data Protection Authority (Bayerisches Landesamt für Datenschutzaufsicht), Promenade 18, 91522 Ansbach, Germany, www.lda.bayern.de.

The app

Lumenkorn has no account, no cloud and no usage statistics. Your photos, the catalog, your edits and all AI results stay on your computer. Lumenkorn only writes to your photo folders when you ask it to (export, merge results, deleting rejected photos).

The app connects to the internet only in these cases:

  • Update check: shortly after starting, Lumenkorn asks GitHub once whether a new version is out. Like any web request, this tells GitHub your IP address.
  • AI downloads: only when you click the button for it, the AI runtime and models are downloaded from the sources named in the README (GitHub, Hugging Face, OpenCV). Nothing about your photos is sent.
  • Map and place names: map images come from OpenStreetMap (tile.openstreetmap.org), place search and place names from Nominatim (nominatim.openstreetmap.org). To find place names, the coordinates of your photos are sent, rounded to about 100 m and bundled per town. Settings → General → Map and place names switches this off completely; the map in the photo album still works because it is built in.

The interface for AI agents (MCP) is off by default. When switched on, only programs on your own computer can connect.
