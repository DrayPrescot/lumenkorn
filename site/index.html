Four cases in which Lumenkorn goes online.

That is all of them. Your photos, the catalog, your edits and all AI results stay on your computer. There is no account, no cloud and no usage statistics.

  • Update check

    GitHubShortly after starting, Lumenkorn asks once whether a new version is out. This tells GitHub your IP address.

  • AI downloads

    GitHub, Hugging Face, OpenCVOnly when you click the button for it. Nothing about your photos is sent.

  • Map and place names

    tile.openstreetmap.org
    nominatim.openstreetmap.org    On a new installation this is off until you switch it on under Settings → General → Map and place names. To find place names, the coordinates of your photos are then sent, rounded to about 100 m and bundled per town.

  • Nextcloud

    Your own serverOnly when you connect it yourself and upload a project's exports or originals. The data only goes to the server you enter.

This website also sets no cookies, uses no tracking and loads nothing from other servers.

From the memory card to the photo book.

1 / 5 Add

Your folders stay as they are.

You add folders, Lumenkorn reads them in place and builds a catalog from them. Files are only copied when you import from a memory card.

  • If you rename or move files, Lumenkorn recognizes them by their content.
  • Projects group folders, even from different drives.
  • Every copy from the memory card is checked byte by byte.

2 / 5 Cull

Get to the photos worth keeping.

The culling assistant suggests what to reject: the weaker shots of a burst, blurry photos, closed eyes, badly exposed photos and photos taken by accident.

  • You check the suggestions, and only what you tick is rejected.
  • Reject a photo with one key and move straight on to the next.
  • Compare up to 12 photos side by side with synchronized zoom.

3 / 5 Develop

The original stays untouched.

Only your settings are saved and the original file stays as it is. For RAW files Lumenkorn follows darktable's scene-referred workflow. Drag the slider in the picture to compare before and after.

  • White balance, exposure, HSL, color grading and tone curve.
  • Auto notes where you differ from its suggestion, separately for each camera.
  • Lumenkorn finds the sky, the main subject, people and animals as masks.
  • The noise reduction removes noise and keeps fine structures such as feathers.
  • With AI repair, dust spots, power lines and signs disappear from the picture.

4 / 5 Find

Search for what is in the photo.

Type “mountains” and you get mountains, even if nobody ever tagged those photos. You download the models for this once, and after that they run offline.

  • Keywords from coarse to fine, such as Mammal → Monkey → Gorilla.
  • Faces are found and grouped in the background.
  • The map shows where your photos were taken. It starts switched off and goes online only once you switch it on.
  • The statistics show which lenses and focal lengths you actually use.

5 / 5 Share

From a trip to a photo book.

Pick a theme and a page size. Lumenkorn chooses the photos and lays them out in the order they were taken. Then change whatever you don't like and save a print-ready PDF.

  • A 300 dpi PDF with bleed, plus every page as a JPG.
  • The slideshow changes photos on the beat of your music and can be saved as an MP4 video.
  • Export to JPEG, 16-bit TIFF or PNG.
  • If you like, upload a project's exports or originals to your own Nextcloud and share them as a link.

HDR, panorama and focus stacking from several photos, also handheld.

Keyboard shortcuts for almost everything, changeable in the settings.

AI agents such as Claude or ChatGPT through a built-in MCP interface, if you switch it on.

English and German throughout the interface.
