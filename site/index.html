Your photos. On your own computer.

Lumenkorn manages your photo collection and develops RAW files. It runs entirely on your computer, so there is no account and no subscription. The AI features run on your machine too, not in someone else's cloud.

Your files stay where they are

Lumenkorn reads your folders directly and never changes the originals. Ratings and edits go into a catalog file on your computer.

RAW development

The processing follows darktable. With a graphics card the sliders respond without a noticeable delay; without one, the processor does the work.

AI without a cloud

Keywords, search by content, masks and noise reduction use open models. You download them once and everything works offline after that.

Library

Your folder structure stays

Add your photo folders and Lumenkorn builds a catalog from them. Rate, flag and tag your photos the way you are used to. Folders that belong together can be grouped into projects, even if they are on different drives.

If you rename or move files, Lumenkorn recognizes them by their content, so ratings and edits are not lost. The file type filter shows, for example, only your RAW files or only the JPEGs.

RAW development

Non-destructive, the way you know it

White balance, exposure, highlights and shadows, dehaze, HSL, color grading and a tone curve. Every change can be undone later, and the original file is never touched.

Auto gives you a first suggestion. Over time it picks up how you like to edit and adjusts to that. Drag the slider in the picture to compare before and after.

Masking

Masks with one click

Lumenkorn finds the sky, the main subject, people and animals. For people you can also select just the hair, eyes or skin. Masks can be combined, for example the sky without the church tower in front of it.

Gradients, brush and color range are there as well, for anything you would rather draw yourself.

Photo album

From a trip to a photo book

Pick a theme and a page size. Lumenkorn chooses the photos and lays them out in the order they were taken. Blurry shots and near-duplicates are left out, and photos with four or five stars are always included. If you like, a map page shows every place of the trip.

Then change whatever you don't like and save a print-ready PDF.

Try it yourself

Lumenkorn is free and the source code is on GitHub. You only download the AI models if you need them.
